NEXORA EMAILJS SETUP
======================

The reservation form is now connected to EmailJS.

1. Create/sign in to an EmailJS account:
   https://dashboard.emailjs.com/

2. Add an Email Service and note its Service ID.

3. Create an Email Template and set the recipient ("To Email") to YOUR email address.

4. In the template, use these variables:
   {{name}}
   {{email}}
   {{model}}
   {{message}}

   Example subject:
   New Nexora Reservation Request — {{model}}

5. In EmailJS Account/Public Keys, copy your Public Key.

6. Open script.js and replace:
   YOUR_PUBLIC_KEY
   YOUR_SERVICE_ID
   YOUR_TEMPLATE_ID

   with your actual EmailJS values.

7. Upload the complete project to GitHub Pages.

IMPORTANT:
- The EmailJS Public Key is intended for browser-side use.
- Do not put an email account password, API secret, or private key into JavaScript.
- The recipient email is configured inside your EmailJS template/service, not in this website code.

FORM FIELDS SENT:
- name
- email
- model
- message
